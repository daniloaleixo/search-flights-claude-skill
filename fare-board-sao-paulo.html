What the board does not know

Every limit that could change a decision, stated rather than smoothed over.

  • Origins backfilled

    BER (8 sweep fares, allows 2 stops against the sweep's 1), HAM (3 sweep fares) were searched alone at their own stop budget. A search is backfilled either because the sweep returned nothing for the origin, or because the origin's stop budget is wider than the single limit a sweep search can carry, so its extra stops were never tried until backfill. Those fares came from a different search than the rest of the board.

  • Coverage

    Every origin returned a usable result page.

  • Rows never expanded

    362 of 462 rows were never opened, so their layovers and their night status are unknown. They show as "not checked", which is not the same as clean: BER 964, AMS 974, FRA 975, AMS 981, FRA 986, FRA 986, FRA 989, FRA 989, FRA 992, FRA 992, FRA 992, FRA 992 and 350 more.

  • Stop limit each search ran under

    AMS at most 1 stop, BER at most 2 stops, FRA at most 1 stop, HAM at most 1 stop, MUC at most 1 stop, PRG at most 1 stop. A sweep carries one stop limit, so it runs at the tightest budget across all origins; an origin allowed more stops only sees them in its own backfill search.

  • No open jaws in this run

    Every fare here is a round trip that comes back to the airport it left from. Open jaws were dropped because Google prices them as multi-city searches, and a multi-city result page carries no Cheapest tab at all, only the Best set. On the one pair measured that gap was 1159 EUR against 879 EUR for the same search. The airport by airport grid therefore only has a diagonal.

  • Self-transfer fares

    10 of 462 rows are self transfers: two separate tickets, so a missed connection is yours to pay for and bags do not through-check. BER 968, BER 1044, AMS 1344, AMS 1406, AMS 1423, AMS 1527, FRA 1608, BER 1641.

  • What a row's link actually opens

    Each row's link is the search it came from, not a bookmark to that one fare: a sweep search carries every origin and one date pair, so opening it reopens the whole result list, and the fare has to be found again inside it, sorted by price. It is not a permalink to a specific itinerary.
